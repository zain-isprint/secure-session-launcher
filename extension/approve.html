If you allow it, pages of this site can ask Secure Session Launcher to:

  • open applications in new normal or incognito windows;
  • close application windows they opened, and clear those applications' cookies and stored data (signing you out of them), when you switch user or sign out of the portal;
  • know which of the application windows they opened is in front.

It cannot read the pages you visit or any passwords. You can remove this approval at any time in the extension's options.
